How true Zero-Knowledge works

1

Local Encryption

When you type a password or API key, SECBASE encrypts it directly inside your browser using your Master Key. Your Master Key is never sent to us.
2

Opaque Transit

Only the heavily encrypted ciphertext leaves your laptop. If anyone intercepts the network traffic, all they see is mathematical gibberish.
3

Blind Storage

Our database stores the encrypted blob. Because we don't have your Master Key, we mathematically cannot decrypt your data. Even if we get hacked, you are safe.
